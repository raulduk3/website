               INT. THE HOUSE - NIGHT

               a phone flashlight. LIAM packs leftovers into containers

               No one answers. Bottom Figure considers this, decides it is
               beneath him, and walks away.

                                   FERNANDO
                         I was cruel.

                                   IRIS
                         Yes.

                                   FERNANDO
                         I heard you.

                                   IRIS
                         Not yet.

               Fernando nods. He stands back and lets Iris choose the
               direction. She starts down the alley. After a beat, she lets
               him walk beside her.

               In the back lot, John finds Miles and Gage where they sleep.
               He brushes the last bright trace from each closed eyelid.

               The four humans remain asleep. John follows Fernando and Iris
               into the thinning dark.



               EXT. BACK STREET / BENCH - MORNING

               A TRASH TRUCK backs up. Its ordinary reverse BEEP wakes the
               city.

               In daylight, the bus bench, red service door, back lot, and
               concrete ramp sit within one very boring block.

               Sophie wakes against the service door. Miles wakes on the
               ramp across from her. He sees her first.

                                   MILES
                         Sophie.

               Sophie gets to her feet. Miles does too, stopping three feet
               away.

                                   MILES
                         I remember parts of it. I remember
                         seeing Julia and thinking I had to--

               He cannot finish the sentence.

                                   SOPHIE
                         You left me sleeping.

                                   MILES
                         I know. I'm sorry. I don't have a
                         version that makes that okay.

                                   SOPHIE
                         Good. Don't make one up.

               Across the block, Julia wakes on the bus bench. Gage climbs
               out of the ditch, covered in mud.

               Gage sees Sophie with Miles. An old reflex brings him half a
               step toward them. It dies there.

               He sees Julia. He does not move until she sees him too.

                                   JULIA
                         Are you done being insane?

                                   GAGE
                         I think so.

               Julia studies him, waiting for the manic certainty to return.
               It does not.

                                   GAGE
                         Last night, I said a lot of things
                         I hadn't earned.

                                   JULIA
                         That is the most normal thing
                         you've said in your life.

                                   GAGE
                         I don't know what last night was.
                         But I know I was looking at Sophie
                         before it. And now I'm looking at
                         you.

               Julia looks past him to Sophie. Then back.

                                   JULIA
                         That's not proof. That's a hangover
                         with a personality.

                                   GAGE
                         Then don't believe me yet. Can I
                         walk with you?

               Julia considers. She points east, toward the lake.

                                   JULIA
                         Lake first. No speeches.

                                   GAGE
                         Okay.

               Gage walks beside Julia, leaving room between them.

               Miles starts after Sophie at the same careful three feet.
               Sophie catches his sleeve and closes one of them.

               The four head east as the city begins its morning.



               EXT. LAKE MICHIGAN - SUNRISE

               The lake is silver. Runners, cyclists, commuters, gulls.
               Downtown brightens across the water like nothing happened.

               The four reach the seawall. Sophie and Miles take one end.
               Julia and Gage take the other. The empty space between the
               sisters is wider than the water looks.

               The wind cuts through Julia's wet clothes. Sophie takes off
               her knit cap, crosses the space, and puts it on Julia's head.

               Julia does not look at Sophie. She pulls the cap down over
               her ears. Sophie returns to Miles.

               Miles holds out a hand but does not reach the last inch.
               Sophie takes it.

               Gage keeps his hands in his pockets. Julia lets her shoulder
               rest against his for one second, then looks back at the lake.

               Far down the breakwater, IRIS and FERNANDO share one paper
               cup of coffee. Iris hooks her hand through Fernando's elbow.
               He follows her pace.

               JOHN stands at the end of the breakwater, small against the
               lake. He looks back at all of them.

                                   JOHN
                         Don't put things in other people's
                         drinks. And don't let your friends
                         get weird alone.

               Whether anyone hears him is impossible to tell.

               The four face the lake as the sun clears the city.

                                                          CUT TO BLACK.
